I've been meaning to start a blog for roughly the entire length of my career. The usual things got in the way: picking the "right" stack, an unhealthy attachment to a perfect first post, and the quiet suspicion that nobody would read it anyway.

So here's the deal I made with myself: **ship something small, write often, fix it later.** This is that something small.

## What this is

`codingdan` is a place for working things out in public — the debugging sessions that ate a weekend, the architecture calls I'd make differently today, and the little tools that quietly made my life better.

I'm not aiming for polished tutorials. I'm aiming for the honest version: what I tried, what broke, and what I'd tell myself if I could go back.

## What to expect

- **Postmortems** of bugs that were far more interesting than they had any right to be.
- **Notes on systems** — backends, infra, and the trade-offs nobody warns you about.
- **Tooling** — the small scripts and setups that compound over time.

If a post here saves you an afternoon of head-scratching, that's the whole point.

## On the stack

This site is built with [Astro](https://astro.build/), deployed to Cloudflare Pages, and styled by hand. I'll write up the build in a follow-up post — there were a couple of decisions worth explaining.
